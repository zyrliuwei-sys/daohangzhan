# Flow TV: free channel surfing, prompts on screen

Flow TV is Google Labs' always-on AI livestream of generated channels — free, no account required, and built on the Veo model family with audio. It runs around the clock at labs.google/flow/tv, and it's the closest thing to old-school channel surfing in the AI era: themed channels, a shuffle mode, and a remote that never runs out of batteries.

The detail that makes it genuinely useful: every clip plays with its prompt on screen. You're not just watching generated video — you're watching what asked it into existence, which turns an idle watch session into a prompting masterclass.

## What's on the stream

- A free, always-on stream of AI-generated video from Google Labs
- Built on the Veo model family, with audio
- Themed channels to hop between, or shuffle everything together
- The prompt behind each clip shown on screen as it plays
- No account required — open and watch (available in supported regions)

## How watching works

1. Open the site to watch Flow TV — the live feed starts immediately, nothing to sign up for.
2. Hop between themed channels, or shuffle the whole catalog into one endless mix.
3. Read the on-screen prompt behind each clip as it plays, and steal the good ones for your own experiments.

There's no voting and no chat to steer — Flow TV is the lean-back entry point among live AI channels, closer to channel-surfing than to directing.

## What makes it different

Most live AI channels ask you to participate. Flow TV doesn't: it's a zero-commitment window into what a frontier model (Veo) produces unprompted by an audience — useful both as background TV and as a study in prompt-to-picture.

It's also the easiest free on-ramp to AI-generated television: no account, no keys, no cost. If you're deciding whether this whole category is for you, this is the cheapest possible test — then graduate to the votable shows when you want your own hand on the wheel.

## Quick answers

### Is Flow TV free?

Yes — it's a free Google Labs experiment, currently available in supported regions. No account is required to start watching.

### What powers Flow TV?

Google's Veo model family generates the channels' video, with audio. Each clip plays with the prompt that produced it shown on screen.

### Can you steer Flow TV like other live AI channels?

Not by voting — Flow TV is built for passive channel surfing: hop between themed channels or shuffle everything together, and read the prompts behind each clip as it plays.
