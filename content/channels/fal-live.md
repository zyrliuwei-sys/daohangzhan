# fal.live: one Director session, a whole audience steering

fal.live is fal.ai's platform for live AI channels — each one a 24/7 AI livestream — summed up by its own tagline: "AI television directed by everyone." Each channel runs a single realtime Director session, broadcast to every viewer at once — so the entire audience watches the same show _and_ writes it together, around the clock.

The architecture is what makes it interesting. One person sets the premise; everyone else directs the scenes. As fal puts it: "No filming, no editing, no render queue."

## What's on the stream

- Live channels built on realtime frontier video models running on fal
- Each channel powered by one realtime Director session, broadcast to all viewers simultaneously
- Channel creators set a resident system prompt — worldview, characters, tone — that directs every scene
- Chat proposes directions and votes; the top-voted prompt becomes the next scene
- Winning scenes generate live within seconds, and channels run 24/7

## How steering works

1. To watch fal.live channels, pick one that's generating now and drop into its world.
2. Propose a direction in chat — or vote for one already on the table.
3. The winning prompt is handed to the Director session, and the next scene renders live within seconds, keeping the resident premise intact.

Because everyone shares one session, there's exactly one show — your vote is competing to write the same stream everyone else is watching. That's the whole game. And every fal.live stream runs 24/7, so the game never pauses.

## What makes it different

A normal livestream transmits a camera's output; fal.live channels have no footage to transmit. The frames are being generated while you watch — which is the only reason a chat vote can change what's on screen seconds later.

And where normal AI video gives you a private clip per prompt, fal.live makes generation a shared, continuous event: one premise, one session, unlimited directors. The resident prompt keeps the thousandth scene inside the world the first scene established. How to write prompts like that is covered in [the AI video director guide](/director).

## Quick answers

### What is fal.live?

fal.ai's platform for live, audience-directed channels. Each channel runs a single realtime Director session that is broadcast to every viewer at once — official tagline: "AI television directed by everyone."

### How does voting on fal.live work?

Viewers propose directions in chat and the room votes. The top-voted prompt becomes the instruction for the next scene, which is generated live within seconds. The channel keeps running 24/7 whether you participate or just watch.

### Who writes the show on fal.live?

Each channel's creator writes a system prompt that acts as its resident director — the worldview, characters, and tone. From then on, that prompt directs every scene while the audience supplies the story beats.
