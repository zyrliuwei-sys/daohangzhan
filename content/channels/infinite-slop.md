# Infinite Slop: the endless show your chat is writing right now

Infinite Slop is an AI livestream in the purest sense: a reality show with no cast, no crew, and no end. Built by Pieter Levels (@levelsio) with fal.ai, it generates its story around the clock on infiniteslop.ai — every frame rendered live by MiniMax H3 running on fal.

The twist is the writers' room. Nobody scripts Infinite Slop; the audience does. Viewers vote on what the characters do next, and the winning call becomes the next scene. Drop in at any hour to watch Infinite Slop — you're not catching up on a broadcast; you're joining the show mid-sentence, with a vote in your hand.

## What's on the stream

- A never-ending, AI-generated reality show, running around the clock
- Story beats chosen by viewer prompts and votes in chat
- Liking and participating earns karma — contributors compete for a cash prize pool ($8,500 at last check)
- Category channels to hop between, including banana, cat, space, news, and game show
- Every frame rendered live by MiniMax H3 running on fal

## How steering works

1. Open the stream and meet the current cast and beat.
2. Vote on what happens next — or type your own direction into chat.
3. Watch the winning call generate on screen, live, and the story roll on without stopping.

There's no render queue and no pause for production: the decision you join becomes the scene you watch.

## What makes it different

A normal livestream shows you what a camera already saw. Infinite Slop has no camera — the model is producing the picture while you watch it, which is why the audience can genuinely steer: the video doesn't exist until the story needs it.

And where normal AI video hands you one finished clip per prompt, Infinite Slop runs a standing premise as one continuous stream — characters, consequences, and all. It's television that is always being decided, which is exactly what makes staying another ten minutes tempting.

## Quick answers

### Is Infinite Slop really endless?

Yes — it streams around the clock, generating its reality-show beats continuously. There are no episodes released on a schedule; the story just keeps producing for as long as the channel runs.

### How do I change what happens on the Infinite Slop stream?

Open the channel, watch the current beat, and vote on what the characters do next — or pitch your own direction in chat. The top decision is rendered into the show live.

### What is karma on Infinite Slop?

Karma is the reward for participating: liking and contributing earns it, and contributors compete for a cash prize pool ($8,500 at last check). Details and current rules live on the official site.
