# Nothing, Forever: the sitcom that never wraps

Nothing, Forever is the original always-on AI sitcom — a low-poly, Seinfeld-style apartment comedy streaming on Twitch since early 2023, with dialogue, jokes, and scenes generated live by AI around the clock. It's one of the longest-running AI livestreams anywhere, and it proved the whole category: a show with no writers' room, no cast, and no reason to ever go off the air.

Tune in to the Nothing, Forever Twitch channel — twitch.tv/watchmeforever — to watch Nothing, Forever at any hour. It has been for years.

## How watching works

1. Open the Twitch channel — the show is already running, whatever the hour.
2. Watch scenes, dialogue, and jokes generate in real time, with stand-up bits between them.
3. Join the chat watching the endless run — part of the fun is the audience's running commentary on what the AI just said.

This is the lean-back format: unlike the votable shows, Nothing, Forever isn't steered by chat. You watch a machine improvise a sitcom, endlessly, and the gamble is whether the next joke lands.

## What makes it different

Every sitcom you've loved had a finale. Nothing, Forever's premise is that there isn't one — the show is a standing format that generates forever, which is why it's the reference point for the whole [AI sitcom generator](/tv-show-generator) idea.

It's also living history: when people ask whether an AI can hold a live audience for years, this channel is the existence proof. Newer shows have fancier models and voting mechanics — this one has a three-year continuous run.

## Quick answers

### Is Nothing, Forever still streaming?

Yes — the channel has kept streaming its AI-generated sitcom continuously since going live in early 2023. It airs around the clock on Twitch at watchmeforever.

### Who writes Nothing, Forever?

AI does — dialogue, jokes, scenes, and even the stand-up bits are generated live. The human team (Mismatch Media) built the show; the machine keeps it on air.

### Why is it low-poly and Seinfeld-style?

That's the premise the show launched with: a blocky apartment-comedy world in the Seinfeld tradition. The standing format is part of what proved an always-on AI sitcom could hold an audience.
