Renoise Live is an AI survival reality show with the format stripped to its bones: four contestants, one storm-bound island, and a live audience deciding what hits them next. The Renoise Live stream runs continuously at renoise.live — every scene generated live, every outcome traceable to a viewer's decision.

Reality TV has always pretended the audience had a say. Here it actually does. The islanders' fate isn't a producers' storyline; it's whatever the chat votes into existence, rendered seconds later.

A normal survival show hands you a cut that was decided in an edit bay months ago. Renoise Live has no edit bay — the picture is being generated as it airs, which is why the audience can be the storm.
