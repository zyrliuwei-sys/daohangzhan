# AI-generated TV: every live channel you can watch right now

**AI-generated TV** is television with no camera behind it: a video model renders the picture as it airs, and on the best channels the audience writes the next scene by voting in chat. This page is the full roster of live channels on air — every entry verified against its official site, every fact current as of September 2026.

## What counts as an AI TV channel

Not every AI video product belongs here. A channel qualifies when:

- the video is **generated live**, not rendered in advance;
- the output is a **continuous stream**, not a downloadable clip;
- the **audience can steer** — by typing, voting, or both;
- a **standing premise** keeps the world and cast coherent for the whole run.

One-shot generators and editing tools fail all four. That's a feature, not a bug — it keeps this list watchable.

## Quick answers

### What is AI-generated TV?

Television where a video model generates the picture as it airs — no camera, no crew, no render queue. Shows run from a standing premise and, on chat-directed channels, the audience votes on every next scene.

### Are these channels really live around the clock?

The ones tagged 24/7 are: Infinite Slop's reality show, Nothing, Forever's sitcom (streaming since early 2023), Flow TV's Veo-powered channels, and the news networks all generate continuously.

### Can I start my own AI TV channel?

Yes — hosted platforms like AITV and Retake.tv launch AI streamers in minutes, and open-source stacks like Infinite TV, SlopTV, AIRI, and AITuber Kit run the whole station yourself. The walkthrough is in the [launch guide](/creators).
