# AI TV show generators: one premise, an endless run

An **AI TV show generator** doesn't hand you a video file. It takes a premise — a world, a cast, a tone — and _runs_ it: scene after scene, generated live, for as long as the channel stays on. Type "the roommate enters with a suspicious haircut" into chat, watch the votes land, and seconds later the show does it. No script department, no filming, no edit. The series is being written the moment you watch it.

If you've only used clip generators (prompt in, video out, repeat), this is a different species — and this page covers both sides: how generated shows actually run, which formats exist today, and how to spin up your own.

## What is an AI TV show generator?

A show generator is a loop, not a button:

1. **The premise** is written once, as a standing system prompt — the show's bible (setting, characters, tone, rules).
2. **Scenes generate continuously** from that premise, live, with no render queue.
3. **The audience steers.** Viewers propose directions; proposals get voted on; the winner becomes the next scene's instruction.
4. **The show never wraps.** There is no finale, no season break — just a continuous stream, 24/7.

The result behaves like television, not like a video tool: you don't produce with it, you _watch_ it — and while watching, you steer.

## How a generated show runs, scene by scene

Take fal.live, the clearest implementation of the pattern. Each channel runs a single realtime Director session that's broadcast to every viewer at once. The channel's creator wrote the resident prompt — worldview, cast, tone. Chat proposes what happens next. The top-voted prompt becomes the next scene, rendered live within seconds. The show's creator never touches a timeline; the audience never stops directing.

Underneath, a realtime video model does the rendering. Infinite Slop's endless reality show is generated by MiniMax H3 running on fal. Flow TV's channels come from Google's Veo models. The open-source station Infinite TV renders Twitch chat into scenes with LTX Video and pushes the result out over RTMP. Different stacks, same loop: premise in, votes in, show out.

## Formats to generate (and where each lives)

### AI episode generator

Want episodes more than a continuous saga? Watch channels that generate in episode-shaped blocks. [Flow TV](/channel/flow-tv) loops AI-generated clips with the prompt behind each one shown on screen — every block is effectively a mini-episode with its script visible.

### AI sitcom generator

The sitcom is the format that proved AI-generated TV first. [Nothing, Forever](/channel/nothing-forever) — a low-poly, Seinfeld-style apartment comedy — has streamed on Twitch since early 2023, with dialogue, jokes, and scenes written live by AI around the clock, stand-up bits included. It remains the reference point for what an always-on AI sitcom can be.

### AI series generator

Serialized drama with stakes that carry over week to week. [Renoise Live](/channel/renoise-live) runs a survival series: four contestants stranded on a storm-bound island, with chat deciding what they face next. [fal.live](/channel/fal-live) hosts creator-defined channels that hold a persistent world and cast. The series never pauses — you just drop into the current episode whenever you arrive.

### Soap-opera arcs that never wrap

The soap opera is the natural AI genre: continuous, melodramatic, allergic to endings. A persistent premise plus a live audience is, functionally, an AI soap opera generator — channels like Infinite Slop build exactly the soap experience: long-running characters, betrayal loops, and cliffhangers that resolve into new cliffhangers, generated for as long as the channel runs.

## Generate your own show

Running your own show is the same loop at smaller scale. Write the premise (world, cast, tone, rules). Pick a runtime: hosted platforms like AITV and Retake.tv get a streamer live in minutes; open-source stacks like Infinite TV and AITuber Kit give you the whole station. Wire the audience in — chat becomes the writers' room. The full walkthrough is in [how to launch an AI channel](/creators), and the creative side of the job — the resident prompt, the voting loop — is covered in [the AI video director guide](/director).

## Quick answers

### Can AI generate a full TV show?

Yes — continuously. Nothing, Forever has streamed an AI-written sitcom on Twitch since early 2023, and channels like fal.live and Infinite Slop generate their shows live, around the clock, with no episodes "finished" in advance.

### What is an AI sitcom generator?

A channel or stack that runs a sitcom premise — cast, apartment, running gags — as a standing prompt and generates dialogue and scenes live. Nothing, Forever is the original: a low-poly, Seinfeld-style sitcom written by AI as it airs.

### Do AI-generated shows stop when I stop prompting?

No. Always-on channels keep generating whether you participate or not — 24/7 is the default. Your prompts and votes steer the story; they don't keep it alive.

### Can I watch AI-generated shows for free?

Often, yes. Flow TV streams 24/7 AI-generated channels free with no account. Other channels and platforms set their own terms — check the official site before signing up.
