# AI tools directory: discover AI tools worth using

This **AI tools directory** collects AI products you can actually use today: video generators, image and design tools, AI assistants and agents, research tools, app builders, voice and music generators, and writing checkers. Every listing explains what the tool does, who it is for, and how to get started, then links to the official site.

## How to use this AI directory

- **Browse by category.** Filter the list by task, such as video, image, research, or coding, to see the tools built for it.
- **Open a product page.** Each page covers features, best use cases, pricing notes, and FAQs.
- **Compare related tools.** Product pages link to similar tools so you can compare before you sign up.

## AI tool categories

### AI video generators

Turn text or images into video, translate and dub existing footage, or edit with AI. Examples include Higgsfield, Kling AI, Luma AI, and Vozo.

### AI image and design tools

Generate images, vectors, and mockups, or convert images into SVG. Examples include Leonardo.Ai, Recraft, and Vectorizer.AI.

### AI assistants and agents

Ask questions with cited answers or hand off whole tasks. Examples include Perplexity and Manus.

### AI research tools

Work from your own sources or from peer-reviewed papers. Examples include Gemini Notebook (NotebookLM) and Consensus.

### AI coding and app builders

Describe an app in plain language and get working, deployable code. Emergent is one example.

### AI voice and music

Generate full songs or real-time speech. Examples include Suno and Cartesia.

### AI writing and detection

Check text for AI generation and plagiarism. Winston AI is one example.

## How we pick tools

We only list usable products, not code-only repositories. Each entry is checked against the official website, and the verification date appears on its product page. Pricing and availability change often, so always confirm current terms on the official site.
